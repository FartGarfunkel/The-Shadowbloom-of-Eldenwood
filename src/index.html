Surviving

There is no hunger. Eating any food heals you on the spot — apples, berries, and roast boar are your lifeline. You have ten hearts, as in the classic game. Heart Crystals (from bosses) add one for good; charms worn in the charm slot add more while you wear them, or guard against fire, water and falls.

Under water, watch your air bubbles; when they run out you drown. Sprint while under water to swim. If you die, your things wait in a grave and you're given a compass that points to it.

At night and in dark caves the monsters come out. Ghouls shamble at you, skeletons keep their distance and shoot, spiders climb walls and pounce, and fuselings creep close, hiss and burst — back away when you hear one. Slimes hop about deep underground. Spiders cling to cave roofs and drop on you from above. A good axe or sword can take a limb off: a ghoul without a leg crawls, a spider short of legs can't leap, a skeleton that loses its bow arm drops the bow (and may try to grab it back). Torches keep the dark away — hold one in your off hand, or hang a torch or lantern on your belt hook (it moves to your backpack when you wear one) and it lights up everything around you as you walk.

Crafting

Crafting works on the grid, the classic way: 2×2 in your inventory, 3×3 at a crafting table. Stuck? Open the recipe book beside the grid — it shows what you can make and can lay the ingredients out for you.

Progression

  1. Punch trees. Chop the base of a tree and the whole thing falls.
  2. Craft a crafting table, then wooden and stone tools.
  3. Smelt iron ore at a furnace. Coal or wood is fuel.
  4. Craft an Ember Effigy and summon the Cinder Golem. Watch the ground — every attack is marked before it lands.
  5. Its Cinder Core makes an Ember Igniter. Light an obsidian frame to enter the Ember Depths and mine cinderite.
  6. A frame of Glow Lanterns filled with water opens the Skylands. Aether crystals there make a Pocket Key — your own private dimension.

Farms and animals

Cows, pigs, sheep and chickens roam the grasslands. Feed two of a kind (wheat, carrots or seeds) and they'll have a baby. Milk cows with a bucket, shear sheep, and carry an animal home with an empty hand while sneaking — animals you set down stay put.

Bone meal (from bones, or a full composter) ripens crops and saplings. Shovels make paths, hoes till soil, and scaffolding lets you climb and sneak back down.

Workstations

Anvils repair and join tools, the loom and smithing table dye and upgrade, the fletching table makes arrows, the stonecutter and sawmill cut blocks. A bow shoots arrows; a spyglass zooms; a compass and clock show their faces on screen when held.

Redstone and rails

Mine redstone ore (it glows when touched) and lay the dust on the ground to carry power from levers, buttons, pressure plates, tripwires and torches. Power fades one step per block of dust; a repeater sends it on at full strength. Redstone opens doors (iron ones need it), lights lamps, fires dispensers and droppers, and moves pistons — sticky ones pull blocks back, and slime or honey drags its neighbours along.

Hoppers pass items between chests and furnaces. A comparator reads how full a container is. Observers pulse when the block they watch changes. Use a repeater to change its delay, a comparator to make it subtract, a daylight detector to invert it.

Rails join up, climb slopes and turn corners as you lay them. Set a minecart on the track and right-click to ride; W gives a push, sneak to get out. Powered rails speed carts up (and stop them when unpowered), detector rails send a signal as a cart passes, activator rails tip riders out. Chest and hopper carts carry things; feed a furnace cart coal and it pushes itself.

World settings and commands

Each world has its own settings (World settings in the pause menu, or More options when you make one): its name, game mode, how long days and nights last, and game rules such as keep inventory, mob griefing and fire spread.

Where commands are on (always in Creative), press T or / to type one: /time set day, /gamemode creative, /tp, /give, /fill and more. Builders get WorldEdit: //wand gives the wand (left click and right click mark two corners), then //set, //replace, //walls, //copy, //paste, //rotate, //stack, //sphere and //undo.

Your worlds are safe

The game saves every few seconds. Worlds keep working through updates, and you can download a backup file from the pause menu at any time. Worlds made before 0.5 keep their terrain as it was; new worlds get the new terrain, caves and biomes. Switch between Survival and Creative from the pause menu.
